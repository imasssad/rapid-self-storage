# Editing your website

A short guide to the website editor for Rapid Self Storage. Every change you save is live on the website within a
few seconds, and you can undo any save.

## Signing in

1. Go to **rapidselfstorage.com/admin**.
2. Enter your email and password, then choose **Sign in**.

You stay signed in on that device for a week. To sign out, choose **Sign out** at the bottom of the menu.

The menu on the left (or **Menu** at the top, on a phone) lists everything you can change. Each screen has a
**Save changes** button at the bottom. Nothing changes on the website until you press it. If you try to leave a
screen with unsaved changes, your browser will warn you first.

## Post a special

1. Choose **Specials**.
2. Switch on **Show the special on the website**.
3. Write a short **Headline** (for example "First month for $1") and, if you like, one sentence of **Details**.
4. Optional: add a button, such as "Reserve now", with the link it should go to.
5. Optional: choose a **First day** and **Last day**. The special appears and disappears by itself on those days
   (California time). Leave both empty to show it until you switch it off.
6. Press **Save changes**.

The special appears in a blue bar at the very top of the website. To take it down, switch it off and save.

## Change a price or mark a size sold out

1. Choose **Unit sizes & prices**.
2. Find the size. Type the **Monthly price** in whole dollars, or leave it empty to show "Call for price".
3. To mark it sold out, switch on **Sold out**. Visitors see it dimmed, with a "Join the waitlist (call)" button
   instead of "Reserve".
4. Press **Save changes**.

You can also add a size (**+ Add a size**), remove one, or change the order with **Up** and **Down**.

## Change your hours

1. Choose **Hours & contact**.
2. **Gate hours:** set when the gate opens and closes.
3. **Office hours:** tick each day the office is open and set the times. If no day is ticked, office hours don't
   appear on the website.
4. Press **Save changes**.

The gate hours appear in several places automatically. Two bits of text also mention them in words: the
"Convenient access" feature and the "When can I get to my unit?" question. If your hours change, update those too
under **Homepage text**.

## Swap a photo

1. Choose **Photos**.
2. Under the photo you want to replace, choose **Choose a new photo** and pick a JPG, PNG or WebP file (up to 8 MB).
   A preview appears, marked "New, not saved yet".
3. Fill in **Describe the photo** in a few words, for example "Drive-up units with blue doors". It helps people who
   can't see the photo, and helps Google.
4. Press **Save changes**.

## Undo a change

1. Choose **Version history**. It lists your last 20 saves: when, who, and what changed.
2. Find the version you want to go back to and choose **Restore this version**, then **Yes, restore**.

The whole website goes back to how it was at that moment. Restoring is saved as a new version too, so if you
change your mind you can restore the one before it.

## Add another person

For example a new office manager or a developer. Only owners can do this.

1. Choose **People**.
2. Under **Add a person**, enter their name and email, and choose what they can do:
   - **Editor:** can change website text, prices and photos.
   - **Owner:** can also add or remove people and change settings.
3. Type a temporary password (or choose **Suggest one**), then **Add person**.
4. Give them the address (rapidselfstorage.com/admin) and the temporary password, in person or by phone. Ask them to
   change it under **Your account** after signing in.

To take someone's access away, choose **Remove** next to their name. There must always be at least one owner.

## If you forget your password

- If someone else is an owner, ask them to open **People** and choose **Reset password** next to your name. They
  give you a new temporary password; sign in with it and change it under **Your account**.
- If you're the only owner, contact your developer. They can reset it for you.

After five wrong passwords within 15 minutes, signing in is paused for 15 minutes for your safety. Wait, then try again.

## Good to know

- **Reviews:** only add real reviews from real customers, with their permission. The reviews section appears on the
  website as soon as at least one review is switched on.
- **Settings** (owners only): choose which email addresses receive messages from the website's contact form.
- **Changing your password** under **Your account** signs you out on your other devices.
