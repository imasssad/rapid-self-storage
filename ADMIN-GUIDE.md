# Editing your website

A short guide to the website editor for Rapid Self Storage. Every change you save is live on the website within a
few seconds, and you can undo any save.

## Signing in

1. Go to **rapidselfstorage.com/admin**.
2. Enter your email and password, then choose **Sign in**.

You stay signed in on that device for a week. To sign out, choose **Sign out** at the bottom of the menu.

## Finding what to change

The editor is laid out like your website.

- **Start here** has a search box: type what you want to change ("price", "phone", "special", "photo", "FAQ") and
  pick a result to go straight to that box. Below it are the most common tasks, and a small picture of your
  homepage: click any part of it to change that part.
- The menu lists **Your homepage, top to bottom**: one screen for each part of the page, numbered in the order
  visitors see them (special offer bar, top of the page, why choose us, unit sizes and prices, and so on down to the
  bottom banner). Each screen has the words *and* the photo for that part.
- **Used all over the site** holds the things that appear in many places: **Hours, phone, address & links**, and
  **All photos** (the four big photos on one screen).
- **Tools**: **Change history & undo**, **Contact form emails** and **People who can sign in** (owners only), and
  **Your account**.

While you're signed in, the website itself shows a small dark bar at the bottom left: **Edit this part: …**. It
names the screen for whatever part of the page you're looking at, and takes you straight there.

## Changing something

Every screen has the boxes on the left and a **Preview** on the right that shows that part of the website as you
type (switch between **Computer** and **Phone**). Visitors don't see anything until you press **Save changes** at
the bottom. **Undo my changes** puts the boxes back the way they were before you started. If you try to leave with
unsaved changes, your browser warns you first.

After saving, **View it on the website** opens that part of the live page, and **Undo this save** takes the save
back if you change your mind.

Long lists (unit sizes, features, questions, reviews…) show each item's name; click an item to open it.

## Post a special

1. Choose **Special offer bar**.
2. Under **Show it on the website**, switch on **Show the special on the website**. Optional: pick a **First day**
   and **Last day** (California time, last day included). Leave both empty to show it until you switch it off.
3. Write a short **Headline** (for example "First month for $1") and, if you like, one sentence of **Details**.
4. Optional: a button label such as "Reserve now". Leave the button link empty to send people to your Reserve page.
5. Press **Save changes**.

The line above the preview tells you whether the special is showing now, starts on a later date, or has ended. To
take it down, switch it off and save.

## Change a price or mark a size sold out

1. Choose **Unit sizes & prices**.
2. In **Prices and sold out**, type the monthly price next to the size (whole dollars), or leave it empty to show
   "Call for price". Switch on **Sold out** to show the size dimmed with a "Join the waitlist (call)" button.
3. Press **Save changes**.

To change a size's width, depth, name or description, add a size or remove one, use **Sizes in detail** below.

## Change your hours, phone or address

1. Choose **Hours, phone, address & links**.
2. **Gate hours:** when the gate opens and closes.
3. **Office hours:** switch on each day the office is open and set the times. Days switched off show "Closed".
4. Change the phone number or address if needed, then press **Save changes**.

These fill in automatically at the top of the page, in the quick facts, the Contact section and the footer. Some
sentences elsewhere on the site were typed by hand and also mention hours, days or the phone number (for example
an FAQ answer). They're listed at the bottom of this screen under **Check text you typed elsewhere**, each with a
link to the box, so you can check they still match.

## Swap a photo

Open the part of the page the photo is in (for example **Top of the page**), or **All photos**.

1. Choose **Choose a new photo** and pick a JPG, PNG or WebP file (up to 8 MB). It shows in the preview straight away,
   marked "New, not saved yet".
2. Fill in **Describe the photo** in a few words, for example "Drive-up units with blue doors". It helps people who
   can't see the photo, and helps Google.
3. Press **Save changes**.

## Undo a change

Right after saving, choose **Undo this save** next to the "Saved" message.

For anything older, choose **Change history & undo**. It lists the last 20 saves with who saved, when, and what
changed (for example "Top of the page: Headline, Introduction").

- **Undo this change** puts back only what that one save changed. Everything else stays as it is.
- **Go back to this version** makes the whole website look exactly as it did right after that save, which also
  undoes every change saved after it.

Either way it's saved as a new version, so you can undo that too.

If two people edit the same part at the same time, the second one to save is told, and can choose to see the other
person's version first or save theirs anyway.

## Add another person

For example a new office manager or a developer. Only owners can do this.

1. Choose **People who can sign in**.
2. Under **Add a person**, enter their name and email, and choose what they can do:
   - **Editor:** can change everything on the website, including prices, specials, photos, hours, phone and links.
   - **Owner:** can also add or remove people and choose where contact form emails go.
3. Type a temporary password (or choose **Suggest one**), then **Add person**.
4. A green box shows the sign-in address, their email and the temporary password, with **Copy these details**. Give
   them to the person; ask them to change the password under **Your account** after signing in.

To take someone's access away, choose **Remove** next to their name. There must always be at least one owner.

## If you forget your password

- If someone else is an owner, ask them to open **People who can sign in** and choose **Reset password** next to
  your name. They give you a new temporary password; sign in with it and change it under **Your account**.
- If you're the only owner, contact your developer. They can reset it for you.

After five wrong passwords within 15 minutes, signing in is paused for 15 minutes for your safety. Wait, then try again.

## Good to know

- **Reviews:** only add real reviews from real customers, with their permission. The reviews section appears on the
  website (between Locations and the FAQ) as soon as at least one review is switched on.
- **Scrolling strip:** switch it off to hide it without losing its phrases.
- Some words are fixed, such as button labels and the small blue labels above headings. Each screen's **Good to
  know** line says what's fixed or set elsewhere.
- **Contact form emails** (owners only): which email addresses receive messages from the website's contact form.
- **Changing your password** under **Your account** signs you out on your other devices.
